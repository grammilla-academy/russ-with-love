A little about me

Hi! I'm Liuba.

A love of language, art, and the conversations that bring us together.

Hi! I'm Liuba, a native Russian teacher from Siberia, now living in Italy.

I graduated from Irkutsk State Pedagogical University and have over 10 years of experience teaching Russian as a foreign language, from complete beginners to advanced learners.

I'm constantly developing my teaching skills through professional training courses and international seminars. My qualifications include TORFL/TRKI Exam Preparation and Organization, a joint program involving the Universities of Parma (Italy) and Saint Petersburg (Russia), as well as Teaching Russian Online, a professional development course offered by the University of Parma.

I also regularly participate in seminars on modern teaching methods and Russian as a foreign language. In March 2025, I led a practical workshop on using artificial intelligence in teaching Russian at the University of Parma.

Beyond teaching, I'm passionate about art, culture, and travel. I also graduated from the Academy of Fine Arts in Bologna, and I love bringing these interests into my lessons.

My approach

Make sense of it.
Then make it yours.

Understand · Practice · Speak

I believe that understanding a language is just as important as practicing it. That's why my lessons combine clear grammar explanations, practical exercises, and real conversation.

Every lesson is adapted to your level, goals, and interests. We move at your pace, focusing on what you need most.

And most importantly, I create a friendly, supportive atmosphere where you can ask questions, make mistakes, and build confidence without pressure.
